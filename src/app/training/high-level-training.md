# High-Level EVS Training Plan

## Overview

This training plan outlines the structure and content of a comprehensive Environmental Services (EVS) training program. It focuses on delivering practical skills and knowledge through a combination of classes, tests, and simulations. The program is designed to meet specific learning objectives and ensure competency in EVS operations.

## Core Principles

This training will utilize Adult Learning principles, to include:

*   **Relevance:** Connect training to real-world EVS tasks.
*   **Experience:** Build upon trainees' existing knowledge and experiences.
*   **Autonomy:** Provide choices and control in the learning process.
*   **Engagement:** Incorporate interactive elements and active learning.
*   **Feedback:** Offer regular and constructive feedback.
* **Collaboration**: foster a team-based approach to learning.

## Training Components

### 1. Classes

#### Module 1: Adult Learning Principles in EVS

*   **Content:**
    *   Overview of Adult Learning Theory (Andragogy).
    *   Applying adult learning principles to EVS training design.
    *   Understanding trainee motivations and learning styles.
    *   Creating a positive and supportive learning environment.
*   **Learning Objectives:**
    *   Define andragogy and its key principles.
    *   Explain how adult learning theory applies to EVS training.
    *   Identify strategies for creating a positive learning environment.
    *   Adjust training style for diverse learning needs.
* **Format:**
    * Lecture
    * Group Discussion
    * Q&A

#### Module 2: Competency Assessment and Documentation

*   **Content:**
    *   Defining and identifying core EVS competencies.
    *   Methods for assessing competency (observation, testing, simulation).
    *   Developing and using checklists and rubrics.
    *   Proper documentation of training and assessments.
    *   Legal and ethical considerations in competency assessment.
*   **Learning Objectives:**
    *   Identify key EVS competencies.
    *   Utilize various assessment methods.
    *   Develop and implement effective assessment tools.
    *   Document training and assessment results accurately.
* **Format:**
    * Lecture
    * Role Play
    * Hands On

#### Module 3: Designing & Delivering Engaging EVS Training

*   **Content:**
    *   Planning effective EVS training programs.
    *   Creating engaging training materials (presentations, handouts, videos).
    *   Interactive and participatory training techniques.
    *   Delivering training effectively.
    *   Evaluating training effectiveness.
*   **Learning Objectives:**
    *   Design a structured EVS training plan.
    *   Create engaging training resources.
    *   Implement interactive training techniques.
    *   Deliver effective EVS training sessions.
    *   Assess training effectiveness and make improvements.
* **Format:**
    * Lecture
    * Group Project
    * Presentation

### 2. Tests

#### Test 1: Knowledge Check

*   **Content:** Covers all content from Modules 1-3.
*   **Format:** Multiple-choice, true/false, short answer.
*   **Learning Objectives Assessed:**
    *   Understanding of adult learning principles.
    *   Knowledge of competency assessment methods.
    *   Ability to design and deliver effective EVS training.
    * Proper Documentation of learning material.

#### Test 2: Practical Application

*   **Content:** Scenarios related to common EVS tasks.
*   **Format:** Written scenarios requiring problem-solving and decision-making.
*   **Learning Objectives Assessed:**
    *   Application of adult learning principles in practical situations.
    *   Competency assessment and documentation skills.
    *   Engagement and effectiveness in training scenarios.

### 3. Simulations

#### Simulation 1: Room Cleaning

*   **Description:**
    *   Trainees will clean a simulated patient room, following all established procedures.
    *   Each room will have different scenarios (e.g., spills, isolation precautions).
*   **Interactivity:** Trainees actively clean the room using provided equipment and supplies.
*   **Hands-On:** Trainees physically perform all cleaning tasks.
*   **Competency Validation:**
    *   Checklists are used to evaluate the trainees' cleaning processes.
    *   Observations are conducted to ensure proper techniques are used.
    *   Scenarios are designed to validate response to different challenges.

#### Simulation 2: Infection Control

*   **Description:**
    *   Trainees will respond to a simulated infection control incident (e.g., spill, outbreak).
    *   Trainees will select proper PPE, and how to appropriately handle the incident.
*   **Interactivity:** Trainees will actively make decisions and take actions to address the scenario.
*   **Hands-On:** Trainees will interact with simulated materials and follow protocols.
*   **Competency Validation:**
    *   Observations are conducted to assess adherence to infection control protocols.
    *   Decisions made during the scenario are evaluated for appropriateness.
    *   Documentation of the incident is reviewed for completeness.

#### Simulation 3: Waste Management

*   **Description:**
    *   Trainees will manage the removal, transport, and disposal of various waste types.
    *   Trainees will interact with simulated waste bins and storage areas.
*   **Interactivity:** Trainees will sort and dispose of waste in a simulated environment.
*   **Hands-On:** Trainees will physically move and handle waste containers.
*   **Competency Validation:**
    *   Checklists are used to ensure correct waste classification.
    *   Observations are conducted to ensure proper handling procedures.
    *   Trainees will be required to demonstrate knowledge of compliance standards.

## Evaluation

*   Post-training surveys to assess trainee satisfaction and program effectiveness.
*   Performance tracking of trainees in their EVS roles after the training.
*   Regular review and updating of the training plan based on feedback and industry standards.
